Mark Ramer Chesed Center

Open and available for people in need in our community — stocked entirely with brand-new items.

The Leon Mayer Fund, in conjunction with The Marion & Aaron Gural JCC, operates this community resource. Everything on the shelves is brand new — nothing here is second-hand, and nobody is asked to feel like a charity case.

The center is named for him

Mark Ramer

Mark Ramer was born on April 22nd, 1947 in Southern Poland, shortly after his parents, Zosia and Leon Ramer, were liberated from a nearby concentration camp after the Holocaust.

He grew up in Poland until the age of ten, then moved to a Displaced Persons camp in Italy, and arrived in America in 1960. He attended Jamaica High School, Queens College, and NYU Dental School, and practiced dentistry for ten years before moving into real estate.

His life's focus was Jewish continuity and community. He served as President of the Jewish Center of Atlantic Beach, and later as President of JCC Global. After visiting Mexico City's Jewish community and seeing its Chesed Center, he came home determined to build the same thing on Long Island.

This is that center.
