Schulder Free Loan Fund

Interest-free loans of up to $7,500 for low- and moderate-income residents of Nassau, Suffolk or Queens counties.

The Schulder Free Loan Fund, at the Hebrew Free Loan Society, provides interest-free loans up to $7,500. The funds cover expenses a borrower cannot cover on their own, or bridge a period of lost income.

Applicants must have an annual pre-tax household income at or below the published limits for their household size. If you are slightly over the limit, contact Loan@HFLS.org to discuss your eligibility with a Loan Officer — it is worth the email.

Apply for a Schulder Free Loan

One important step when you apply

On the online application, select "Other" and write "Schulder Fund" in the detail field. That is what routes your application to this fund.

Repayment terms

  • Loans of $5,000 or less — $250 per month over 20 months
  • Loans over $5,000 — $300 per month over 25 months
  • All loans include a three-month grace period
  • Repayments are debited electronically from your checking account on the 5th or the 20th of the month — your choice

What you'll need

From you, the borrower

  • A completed loan application
  • Government-issued photo ID with signature, plus proof of current address
  • A voided check or bank document showing your account details

From your guarantors

  • A completed Guarantor Form
  • Government-issued photo ID with signature

How the process runs

  1. Submit your documents. Send everything before your interview — it is the single biggest cause of delay.
  2. A 15-minute interview with an HFLS Loan Officer, by phone or video call (Zoom).
  3. Review. HFLS reviews and decides on applications weekly. You'll hear by email within one week of your interview.
  4. Signing. If approved, you'll get DocuSign instructions for a Promissory Note (signed by borrower, guarantor and spouses) and an Authorization of Automatic Payments form.
  5. Funds. Money is disbursed within three business days of receiving the signed documents.

Questions? Hebrew Free Loan Society — Loan@HFLS.org · (212) 687-0188

About the Hebrew Free Loan Society

The Hebrew Free Loan Society fosters financial stability by providing interest-free loans that enable borrowers to attend college, improve job skills, start businesses, or handle unexpected financial challenges. Operating since 1892, HFLS serves the New York City area on a non-sectarian basis.

The fund is named for him

Howard Schulder

In 2021, the Schulder Free Loan Fund was founded and funded by Howard Schulder to provide interest-free loans to people in need. The Fund is the fulfillment of a vision Howard developed over 45 years working in the asset-based lending business.

It serves residents of the Five Towns, Far Rockaway and surrounding areas who face financial hardship. To keep costs low while maintaining credibility, operations run under the Leon Mayer Fund, an established local charity, and the Hebrew Free Loan Society manages every loan process from application through repayment tracking.

Howard's goal is to provide numerous loans to people to overcome sudden financial hurdles that arise.

As borrowers repay their loans, that same money cycles out to new applicants — again and again. Howard encourages anyone eligible to apply. And anyone who wants to widen the fund's reach can make a tax-deductible contribution to it.
