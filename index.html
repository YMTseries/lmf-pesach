What we do

Many things, for many families.

Emergency help for newlyweds, single-parent families, and people who have just lost a job — plus the programs below, running all year.

🏠

Mark Ramer Chesed Center

A community store stocked with brand-new clothing, shoes, baby items, household goods, electronics and toys — free to families who need them.

Schulder Free Loan Fund

Interest-free loans up to $7,500 for residents of Nassau, Suffolk and Queens — administered with the Hebrew Free Loan Society.

Pesach food distribution

Every year we subsidize Yom Tov meals for thousands of families across numerous communities, packed and distributed by community volunteers.

Food & clothing subsidies

Ongoing support for families who are stretched thin, delivered discreetly so that no one has to choose between eating and their dignity.

💳

Credit counseling

We negotiate with lenders on behalf of families buried in credit card debt — free of charge — and teach young couples budgeting and credit basics.

🎓

Camp, school & medical help

Camp tuition assistance, school placement, medical assistance, help with family issues, and emergency loans where a guarantor is available.

The man behind it

Rabbi Simcha Lefkowitz

Rabbi Lefkowitz founded the Leon Mayer Fund more than thirty years ago and has administered it ever since — without ever taking a salary. He is Rabbi Emeritus of Congregation Anshei Chesed in Hewlett, a departmental chaplain for the Nassau County Police Department, and co-founder of LIFE, Inc.

He has run all of it from a wheelchair for the past twenty-five years.
